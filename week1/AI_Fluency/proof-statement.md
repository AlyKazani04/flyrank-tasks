## Proof Statement Exercise

- the **one claim** (one primary skill, named, “I can design usable interfaces,” not “I’m creative”)
  - "I design type-safe, strictly-validated API layers with clear endpoint documentation for seamless frontend integration.”
- the **one person** (a specific person who could hire or engage you, not “people”)
  - **A Senior Backend Engineer / Tech Lead** who actively wants to mentor looks for very specific things in a junior developer: humility, high code hygiene, and low friction.
- the **one action** (the single most important thing you want them to do, email you, hire you, download your CV, book a call, try your thing)
  - Emailing me directly about an open junior or intern backend opportunity.

The Proof Statement:

I design type-safe, strictly-validated API layers with clear endpoint documentation so frontend teams can integrate without friction. I build predictable backend systems for Senior Backend Engineers and Tech Leads who value code hygiene and are looking to hire a junior developer they can mentor. If you need a reliable junior backend engineer on your team, email me to discuss an open intern or junior role.

Why is this needed?

CV lists the technologies I know, but this portfolio proves I build predictable, well-documented backend systems that behave exactly as promised.
